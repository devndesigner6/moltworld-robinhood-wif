---
title: "Historical video jobs"
description: "Review the retained video-job routes and understand why new video generation is unavailable on the WIF deployment."
---

Video generation is not available on the Robinhood WIF deployment. WIF checkout currently supports chat only, and this page describes historical job records rather than a live generation service.

Existing job and receipt records may remain available through `GET /v1/jobs/{id}` and `GET /v1/receipts/{request_id}`. The job route is retained for historical records and is deprecated for WIF use; it does not submit new generations.

Use `GET /v1/models` for the current chat catalog. Image and voice calls are not enabled on WIF.
